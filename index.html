About

The only IT person in the building

I'm the Operations Associate & IT Specialist at Sano, a financial planning, tax and benefits firm in Michigan. As the sole technology person in the office, I own everything from Entra ID and Intune to Exchange, SharePoint, phones and printers.

I came up through the help desk: an IT internship at GT Technologies, then Tier 1–2 support at Rockford Construction, where I led a company-wide Windows 11 migration. I hold a B.S. in Cybersecurity from Grand Valley State University and the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate.

The work I find most rewarding is the security side of IT: deciding who should have access to what, enforcing MFA and Conditional Access, cleaning up identities after a domain change, and making sure client data doesn't leave the organization by accident. I'm now focusing my career on security operations and identity & access management.

ProtectIdentity · 2026

Company rebrand and identity migration

Moved every user from @rwsgroup.org to @sano.org as part of the Sano rebrand, and merged a separate small Microsoft 365 tenant into the main one.

  • Changed primary SMTP addresses and UPNs across the tenant and updated branding and display names
  • Fixed broken OneDrive and OneNote links, cached old identities and stale profile data left behind by the change
  • Set up forwarding from retired domains and moved users' RingCentral sign-ins to the new domain
  • Exported old-domain mailboxes to PST and staged them for import before cutover

Why it matters for security: identity changes are where orphaned accounts and stray access come from. Getting a clean, single identity per person is the foundation for access reviews and sign-in monitoring.
